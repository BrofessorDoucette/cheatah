Struct

space::irbem::T01sGeometry

cheatah-space v0.1.0-alpha — Biome Standard 0.6.4-alpha

The current-sheet geometry shared by every disc and sheet mode, held fixed across all driver states.

These are the non-linear parameters of the Tsyganenko (1989) sheet construction — the hinge, the warp, the truncation — chosen once from that paper's fitted range (its R_c, G, x_0, D_y columns of Table 1 bracket the values here) and then HELD, so that every driver dependence of the model is carried by the linear amplitudes and the field stays linear in the calibrated coefficients. Moving the sheet with the drivers is what the 2003 paper does for its tail inner edge; here the same effect is spanned by discs at several radial scales instead, which is what keeps the amplitude solve linear.
