Struct

space::irbem::api::MagneticCoordinates

cheatah-space v0.1.0-alpha — Biome Standard 0.6.4-alpha

The six per-point outputs of IRBEM's MAKE_LSTAR, in one record.

IRBEM returns them as six parallel arrays. They are one array of records here because the six are computed together from one drift shell, and six spans a caller has to keep in step is six chances to hand one of them the wrong length.
