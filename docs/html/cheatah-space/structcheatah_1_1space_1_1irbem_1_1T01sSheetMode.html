space::irbem::T01sSheetMode

cheatah-space v0.1.0-alpha — Biome Standard 0.6.4-alpha

One equatorial radial-current sheet mode — the partial ring current's closure system.

The curl of Lambda z_hat with Lambda = G(rho) H(z_r) q, G = (rho^2 + a^2)^(-3/2), H = z_r / sqrt(z_r^2 + d^2) and q an azimuthal weight. A field with no z component in the sheet's own frame, that flips sign across the sheet — which is the field of a thin sheet of current flowing RADIALLY in the equatorial plane, inward on one side and outward on the other for q = y. Exactly divergence-free because it is a curl, whatever G, H and q are.
