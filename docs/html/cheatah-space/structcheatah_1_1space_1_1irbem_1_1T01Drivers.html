space::irbem::T01Drivers

cheatah-space v0.1.0-alpha — Biome Standard 0.6.4-alpha

The six drivers T01 reads, in IRBEM's maginput units.

G1 and G2 are the hour averages defined in IRBEM's maginput table and P2 eqs. (1) and (4): G1 = <V h(B_perp) sin^3(theta / 2)> with h = (B_perp / 40)^2 / (1 + B_perp / 40), and G2 = 0.005 <V B_s> with B_s the southward IMF magnitude. They are integrals over history, so they are inputs here, never derived from the instantaneous B_y, B_z.
