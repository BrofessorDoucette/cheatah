space::irbem::T01PositionFold

cheatah-space v0.1.0-alpha — Biome Standard 0.6.4-alpha

The batch's position caveat, accumulated one point at a time — T89PositionFold's shape for T01's envelope, which is a tailward plane rather than a radius: the smallest x_GSM and the smallest r decide the batch, and the radius is compared as a square, so there is no per-point sqrt.
