space::irbem::T01sDiscMode

cheatah-space v0.1.0-alpha — Biome Standard 0.6.4-alpha

One current-carrying disc mode.

The three disc potentials are Tsyganenko (1989) eqs. (7)-(9): with xi = sqrt(z_r^2 + D^2), u = a + xi, S = sqrt(rho^2 + u^2), family 1 is P1 = 1/(S + u) (the paper's A^(1), the field of a thin disc), family 2 is P2 = dP1/da = -1/(S (S + u)) and family 3 is P3 = dP2/da = 1/S^3 — the only one with a finite magnetic moment, hence the ring current. P is the potential over radius: A = P (-y, x, 0). The azimuthal weight multiplies P INSIDE the potential, so the field stays exactly divergence-free whatever the weight.
