space::irbem::T01UnitConstants

cheatah-space v0.1.0-alpha — Biome Standard 0.6.4-alpha

The two unit constants the papers omit — MEASURED against the oracle, and stated as such.

Both papers print their building blocks without the constant that turns them into nanotesla. P1 ¶35 normalizes the Birkeland currents to "10^6 A per half-wave of each sinusoidal mode" and Table 2 gives B_m of order 0.1-0.7, but eqs. (16)-(17) as printed have no unit factor, and with B_m alone the Region 1 field would be ~0.1 nT where Table 2's own <B> column says ~13 nT. The tail disc of eqs. (2)-(5) with Table 1's f_i gives ~-30 nT per unit amplitude at the Earth for the short module, while P2 ¶50's worked example puts the WHOLE shielded tail at -22.8 nT there with t_1 ~ 4.7. So each family carries one scalar that P2's fitted amplitudes were expressed against and that the text does not state.

Each is measured ONCE, as a least-squares ratio against the oracle's response to the driver that isolates that family in P2 eq. (11) — G1 drives only the two tail amplitudes, G2 drives the four Birkeland amplitudes and two small geometric factors — over 746 points in 3 <= r <= 15 R_E, x >= -14 R_E, by tools/oracle/t01_diff.cpp (its "response" pass reproduces the numbers):

  • tail: fitting a_1 B_T1 + a_2 B_T2 to dB/dG1 gives a_1 = 0.02309 against the published t_1^(2) = 0.319, i.e. 0.0724 per unit amplitude, with a 37.2% residual; the long module's fitted weight is indistinguishable from zero (a_2 = 0.0006) because its UNSHIELDED field — a current ring stretched to four times the disc, enclosing the entire magnetopause — is not a proxy for the confined field the oracle carries (see the file brief). The one constant is applied to both modules; the differential section says what that costs.

  • Birkeland: fitting the four modes with the published amplitude ratios of P2 Table 1 to dB/dG2 gives a common scale of 673 with a 47.1% residual; freeing all four amplitudes lowers it only to 46.4%, with per-mode scales of 695, 480, 734 and 554 — the published ratios are consistent with the oracle to the ~20% the missing shielding leaves unexplained.

A single scalar per family cannot repair a wrong spatial pattern: the pattern agreement in those fits is what validates the implementation, and the scalar is the unit.
