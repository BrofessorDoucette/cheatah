space::irbem::T01Cone

cheatah-space v0.1.0-alpha — Biome Standard 0.6.4-alpha

One Birkeland mode's published parameters — P1 Table 2, one column: the conical amplitude B_m, the cone's apex colatitude theta_0 (eq.

15, Fig. 3), and the 29 coefficients of the (r, theta) deformation, eqs. (18)-(19).

Unit testIrbemT01.PublishedTablesAreTranscribed

Constants & variables

var int m #

Which Fourier mode, m = 1 (dawn-dusk antisymmetric) or m = 2; the exponent in eq. (17).

var double b_m #

B_m, the mode's overall magnitude coefficient, P1 Table 2.

var double theta_0 #

theta_0, radians — the colatitude of the cone's sheet at the ionosphere.

Delta theta, radians — half the sheet's angular thickness (P1 ¶36: 3.5 degrees for Region 1 and 5.2 for Region 2 in total).
