space::irbem::T96Geometry

cheatah-space v0.1.0-alpha — Biome Standard 0.6.4-alpha

The non-linear geometry of the basis — the numbers the linear fit cannot move.

Everything the disc modules, the field-aligned-current sheets and the box harmonics need beyond their amplitudes. The sheet geometry (r_c .. d_y) is T89's (eqs. 11 and 13) and takes T89's meaning; the scale-length ladders are the disc, shell and box scales the basis is evaluated at. Templated on nothing: the fp32 lane reads these through static_cast<T> exactly as it reads t89_fixed.
