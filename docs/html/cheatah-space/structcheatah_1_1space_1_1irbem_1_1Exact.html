space::irbem::Exact

cheatah-space v0.1.0-alpha — Biome Standard 0.6.4-alpha

The fp64 reference lane: everything in double.

What the other lanes are measured against, and the only lane whose disagreement with IRBEM is attributable to algorithm rather than to arithmetic. Slower on the GPU by roughly the ratio of fp64 to fp32 throughput, and on much consumer hardware not runnable there at all.
